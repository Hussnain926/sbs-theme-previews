    Caring for a leather collar so it lasts ten years

    By the Kindred workshop · Sample article

    Vegetable-tanned leather gets better with age. It darkens, softens and takes the shape of your dog's neck. It only needs a little care to stay that way.

    Clean it gently

    Wipe off mud with a damp cloth after muddy walks. Never put leather in the washing machine and never dry it on a radiator. Let it dry slowly at room temperature.

    1Wipe with a damp cloth
    2Dry slowly, away from heat
    3Condition every 3 months
    Treat it like a good pair of boots, and it will outlast them.

    Condition it a few times a year

    A thin layer of beeswax balm keeps the fibres supple. Rub it in with your fingers, wait ten minutes and buff with a soft cloth.
