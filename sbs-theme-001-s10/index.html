A day with Rosa

Words by the Cobble team · Photos in Porto

Rosa's sun room, 9am

Rosa starts every morning the same way: a small coffee, the radio on and a basket of wool at her feet. She has been knitting for 31 years and can finish a chunky cardigan in about four evenings.

31 yearsof knitting
18 hoursper cardigan
4 coloursshe loves most

Why hand-knit still matters

A machine can make a jumper in minutes. But a hand-knit piece has a softer drape, a little character and a story you can feel. That is why every Cobble label carries the maker's name.

"I want people to feel looked after when they wear it."

Next time you button up your cardigan, think of Rosa's sun room, and the radio playing softly in the background.

Our story

A little shop with a lot of heart

Cobble began on a cobbled street in 2019, with one rail of hand-knits and a pot of tea for anyone who stopped by.

Today we work with twelve home knitters and a handful of small workshops. We still make everything in small batches, and we still sign every label.
