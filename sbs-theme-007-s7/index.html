    Setting up a home for an older cat

    By the Kotatsu workshop·November 2026

    Cats over ten slow down quietly. They still want the high spot and the warm spot, but the jump costs more. A few changes at home let them keep their routines without strain.

    Lower the way up

    Add a step halfway to favourite places: a low shelf by the window, a stool next to the sofa. Wall steps work well when the gap is no more than 30 cm.

    Keep the old favourite places. Just make the route there shorter.

    Warm, low and firm

    Older joints like a bed with a low entry and a thick, firm cushion. Place it away from draughts and close to where you sit in the evening.

    Workshop tipPut a second water bowl or a fountain on each floor of the home. Older cats drink more when water is close.

    A short checklist

    1. A bed with a rim under 10 cm high.
    2. One step to every high favourite place.
    3. Water in two places, away from food.
    4. Ten minutes of slow play each day.

    None of this needs a full redesign. Start with the spot your cat already loves and make it easier to reach.

    Common questions

    How long do made to order pieces take?
    Oak beds and trees are made in 3 to 4 weeks, then ship in 2 to 4 days. You get an email when your piece leaves the workshop.
    My cat ignores the new bed. What now?
    Put it where your cat already sleeps, add a worn t-shirt and a pinch of silvervine. If it still is not used after 60 days, return it for free.
    Can I replace the sisal rope?
    Yes. Replacement rope and cushion covers are sold separately and fit in a few minutes with the included key.
    Do the trees fix to the wall?
    Pagoda Mid and Tall come with a wall strap and fixings. We recommend using it in homes with more than one cat.
