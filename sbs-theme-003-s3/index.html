The four-step routine, explained.

By the Oriel team · October 2026

Most skin does best with a short routine done consistently. Four steps cover what skin needs: clean it, hydrate it, protect the barrier, and shield it from the sun.

1. Cleanse, gently

A low-foam gel cleanser removes sunscreen and oil without stripping. If your skin feels tight afterwards, your cleanser is too strong.

Gel Cleanser
150 ml · $24

2. Hydrate with a serum

Hyaluronic acid draws water into the skin. Apply it to damp skin and seal it in straight away.

"Consistency beats intensity. The best routine is the one you actually do every day."

3. Protect the barrier

A ceramide moisturiser keeps water in and irritants out. This step matters most for dry and sensitive skin.

4. Finish with SPF

Daily sunscreen is the single most effective step for long-term skin health. Use it every morning, rain or shine.
