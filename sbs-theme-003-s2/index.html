LIPS · 4 MIN

The 3-step red lip that never bleeds

By the Swatch team

A red lip looks bold, but the trick is calm prep. Three products and two minutes is all it takes.

1. Line first

Trace the outer edge with a liner one shade deeper, then fill the whole lip lightly. This stops colour from moving.

Line Up Liner
Red · $14

2. Two thin layers

Apply lipstick, blot once, then add a second thin layer for colour that lasts.

"Blot between layers. It is the whole secret."

3. Gloss in the centre

Tap a little clear gloss in the middle of the bottom lip for a fuller look.
