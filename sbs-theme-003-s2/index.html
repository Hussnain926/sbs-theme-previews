How to find your undertone in 60 seconds

Depth is how light or dark your skin is. Undertone is the colour under the surface. Get both right and a tint disappears into your skin.

Three quick checks

Look at your wrist in daylight. Hold a white and a cream sheet of paper by your face. Think about whether gold or silver jewellery looks better on you.

Blue veins and silver: cool. Green veins and gold: warm. Both: neutral.

And olive?

Olive skin has a green-grey cast. Regular warm shades can look orange on it, so we make a separate olive range.
