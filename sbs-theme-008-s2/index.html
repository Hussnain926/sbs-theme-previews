How to pack two weeks in one carry-on.

By the Riva teamJune 2026

The best holidays start with a light bag. After many summers of ferries, steep stone steps and tiny hotel wardrobes, we pack the same way every time: one colour story, ten pieces and nothing that needs ironing.

Start with one colour story

Pick two calm base colours and one bright one. Ours is ivory and navy with a little lemon. Everything goes with everything, so ten pieces become more than twenty outfits.

“If it does not go with three other things in the case, it stays at home.”

The ten pieces

  • One linen shirt dress, for breakfast, beach and dinner.
  • Two shirts: one plain, one stripe.
  • Wide linen trousers and one midi skirt.
  • Two swimsuits, so one is always dry.
  • One kaftan, one hat, one pair of sandals you can walk in.

Let linen crease

Linen is meant to look lived in. Roll each piece instead of folding it, hang it in the bathroom while you shower and the steam does the rest.

How does Riva linen fit?
Most pieces are relaxed. If you are between sizes, take the smaller one, or use the fit finder on any product page.
Will the linen shrink?
No. Every piece is stone washed before sewing, so it will not shrink in a 30°C wash.
How do I care for swimwear?
Rinse in cold fresh water after the sea or pool and dry in the shade. Our recycled yarn resists chlorine and salt.
Can I return a worn piece?
Yes, within 60 days, as long as it is clean. Swimwear needs its hygiene liner in place.
