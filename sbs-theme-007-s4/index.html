      How to throw a dog birthday party

      Dogs do not count birthdays, but they love the attention. Keep the party short, the guest list small and the cake dog-safe.

      Pick a cake they can eat

      Skip human cake. Chocolate, raisins and the sweetener xylitol are dangerous for dogs. Choose a pupcake made for them, with a yogurt frosting and a short ingredient list.

      Party plan

      You need
      • A 4 inch pupcake per 2 dogs
      • Water bowls for guests
      • A quiet room to rest
      On the day
      1. Short walk before guests arrive
      2. Cake in small slices, one each
      3. Photos, then nap time
      Cut the cake into small slices and serve it as a treat, not a meal.

      Mind the guest list

      Invite dogs who already know each other. Keep toys away during cake time to avoid squabbles, and check every guest's allergies first.
