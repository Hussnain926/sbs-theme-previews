The five-minute pour over, step by step

By the Marigold roasting team·6 min read

Pour over sounds fussy. It is not. Once you have the ratio and a steady pour, it takes five minutes and tastes clearer than any machine at home.

Recipe card

20 gCoffee
320 mlWater
Medium-fineGrind
3:00Time

What you need

A dripper and a paper filter, a kettle (a gooseneck makes it easier), a scale and fresh coffee. Water just off the boil, around 94°C.

The steps

  1. Rinse the filter with hot water and pour it away. This warms the dripper and removes any paper taste.
  2. Add 20 g of coffee, ground like coarse sand. Shake it flat.
  3. Pour 50 ml of water and wait 30 seconds. This is the bloom: the coffee releases gas and puffs up.
  4. Pour the rest in slow circles, in two or three stages, until you reach 320 ml.
  5. Let it drain. Total time should be close to three minutes. Faster? Grind finer. Slower? Grind coarser.
If it tastes sour, grind finer. If it tastes bitter, grind coarser. That one rule fixes most cups.

Which coffee to use

Lighter roasts shine here. Try Golden Hour for peach and jasmine, or Sunday Best if you like it sweet and round.
