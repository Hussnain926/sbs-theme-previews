Every product has a fit tip next to the size buttons. Jackets run roomy, so go down one if you are between sizes.

Our canvas is pre-shrunk, so expect under 2% shrink on the first cold wash.

How do free repairs work?

Email us a photo, we send a prepaid label, and we return it fixed within three weeks.

Can I return worn items?

Unworn items with tags can be returned within 30 days for a full refund.

Three years in the same jacket

12 September 2026 · By Sam Hale · 6 min read

When the Chore Jacket arrived it could almost stand up on its own. Twelve ounces of unwashed canvas is stiff, dark and a little noisy. That is the point: it has nowhere to go but towards you.

Months one to six

The first creases set at the elbows and across the lap where it sat folded in the car. By month four, the phone pocket had a pale rectangle and the cuffs had started to soften.

Year two

The first wash, cold and inside out, took the edge off the stiffness without losing the colour. The collar began to fade to a warm clay, and the corozo buttons wore a faint ring into the placket.

Year three

One small tear at the pocket corner came back from our repair service in eleven days, patched in matching canvas. The jacket now fits like it was made for one person, because by now it has been.

Fewer pieces, heavier cloth

Harvest Row started in 2019 with one jacket and a problem: everything we bought wore out in two winters. So we went looking for the mills that still weave cloth slowly, on old looms, at weights the high street stopped using.

We make small runs, so nothing ends up in a landfill sale. And we repair anything we sell, for free, for as long as you wear it.

The cloth

Canvas from Okayama, jersey from Porto, twill from Biella. We visit every mill before we buy a metre.

The cut

Patterns are cut by hand in runs of 200 or fewer, so every seam lines up with the grain.

The finish

Flat-felled seams, bar-tacked stress points and copper rivets. Built so we rarely need to use our repair promise.
