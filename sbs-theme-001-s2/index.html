How to fade denim honestly

By Ruth, third generation

Good denim tells your story. The fades come from the way you sit, bend and reach, not from a factory. Here is how we look after ours.

Wear it more than you wash it

Indigo leaves the cotton slowly, with friction. Wear the jacket every day for the first six months and only spot clean it.

"A jacket that has been mended twice is worth more than one that never was."

When it does need a wash, turn it inside out, soak it cold for an hour and hang it to dry. The fades stay sharp and the wool lining stays soft.

Our story

Eighty years on the row

In 1946 our grandparents opened a dry goods counter on Harvest Row, selling work shirts and boots to the farm families nearby.

We still weave our own flannel, line our jackets with wool blankets and cut each belt from a single hide. And we still mend everything we make, for life.
