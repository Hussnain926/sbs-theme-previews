    Setting up a home practice corner you will actually use

    By the Stillform team · Sample article

    The best practice space is the one you walk past every day. It does not need to be big. It needs a little light, enough floor for your mat and a place to keep your props so that starting takes ten seconds, not ten minutes.

    Start with the floor

    Wood or a low rug works best. Leave room for your arms out to the side and a step in front of the mat.

    2 mof clear floor length
    1good mat that stays out
    2props within reach
    Make starting easier than skipping.

    Keep props close

    A basket for a block and a strap, a bolster by the wall. When the props are visible, you use them.

    Light and quiet

    Morning light is best for waking practice. For evening, use a warm lamp and put your phone in another room.
