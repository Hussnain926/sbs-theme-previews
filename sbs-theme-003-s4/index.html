A 3-drop face oil ritual for dry autumn skin

By the Fernwild farm team

When the air turns cold, skin loses water fast. A face oil on damp skin locks it back in. Here is the simple ritual we use on the farm.

1. Mist first

Spray Rosemary Mist on clean skin. Oil works best on damp skin.

Rosemary Mist
100 ml · $24

2. Warm three drops

Warm three drops of Meadow Face Oil between your palms for a few seconds.

Press, don't rub. Pressing spreads the oil without pulling the skin.

3. Seal the edges

Finish with a little balm on dry spots: the sides of the nose and the corners of the mouth.
