How to hang a gallery wall that lines up

14 Oct 2026 / By Mae Okoro / 6 min read

Start on the floor. Lay your frames out on a sheet of paper the size of the wall, keep a 6 cm gap and trace each one. Tape those templates to the wall before you hammer a single nail.

Pick the centre line

Hang the middle row at 145 cm from the floor to its centre. Eyes land there in most rooms, and the rest of the wall builds up and out from that line.

Check the maths

On a 240 cm wall with 50 cm frames and 8 cm gaps, four frames fit with 8 cm spare. Our planner on the home page does this sum for you.

Fix and level

Mark nail points through the paper, tear it away, then hang. A small spirit level on the top edge of each frame keeps the row true.
