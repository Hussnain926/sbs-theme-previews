    How to hang art above a low bench

    By the Mortise studio

    A low bench and one large print is the simplest way to give a room a centre. The trick is space: let the wall do half the work.

    1. Mind the height

    Hang the print so its centre sits about 25 cm above the bench, or 150 cm from the floor if the wall is empty.

    Leave one hand of empty wall on each side. Calm needs room.

    2. Pick one size up

    Most prints are hung too small. The print should be about two thirds of the bench width.

    3. Add one object

    A single raw clay vase with a dried branch links the wall to the floor.
