    How to order kit for a whole squad without the stress

    By the Pitchside team · Sample article

    Ordering kit for twenty players is not hard, but it is easy to get one detail wrong. A missing size or a misspelled name can hold up the whole order. Here is the checklist we give every club manager.

    1. Collect sizes early

    Send a simple form to parents or players at least four weeks before the first match. Ask for chest size, not age, and add one spare in the two most common sizes.

    4 weeksbefore kick-off
    +2spare shirts
    1list for names and numbers
    Check every name twice. Printed shirts can't be returned.

    2. Lock names and numbers

    Keep one shared list with name, number and size for each player. Numbers 1 to 25 cover most squads. Use capitals and check spelling with each player.

    3. Order once, deliver by team

    One order with split delivery saves money on shipping and keeps all invoices in one place for the club treasurer.
