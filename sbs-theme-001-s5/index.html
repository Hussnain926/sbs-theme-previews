How to break in duck canvas

// Words by Dana, head of product

Good duck canvas starts stiff. That is the point: tight cotton weave that shrugs off nails, branches and concrete. Here is how to get it soft faster.

  1. Wear it, a lot. Body heat and movement do more than any wash.
  2. One cold wash. Inside out, no softener, then hang dry.
  3. Work the creases. Roll the cuffs and collar by hand for a minute a day.

What not to do

Skip the hot tumble dryer. It shrinks the canvas and bakes in creases you do not want.

"Stiff on day one. Perfect by week four."

And if a seam or rivet ever gives, send it back. We repair them for life.
