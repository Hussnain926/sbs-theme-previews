Styling

How to layer pattern without chaos

By the Plinth studio · 5 min read

A room full of pattern can feel warm and collected, or loud and busy. The difference is a few simple rules about scale and colour.

Start with the biggest pattern

Your rug sets the tone. Choose it first, then pick cushions and throws in smaller prints that share one or two of its colours.

"One big pattern, two small ones, and a colour that ties them together."

Finish with plain textures, like a knitted pouf or a linen throw, so the eye has somewhere to rest.
