Styling

How to light a dark room

By the Kiln design studio · 6 min read

Dark walls make a room feel calm and close. They also swallow light. The answer is not more light but light at more heights.

Three heights of light

Start with a pendant over the table or the centre of the room. Add a floor lamp by the chair you read in. Finish with a small lamp at the level of a sideboard or a bedside table.

"Brass does not just hold the light. It gives it back, warmer."

Choose warm bulbs (2700K) and put every lamp on a dimmer. In the evening, turn the pendant down and let the lower lamps lead.
