How to size a rug

12 October 2026 / By Maya Reed / 5 min read

Most rugs are bought too small. The room looks like it is floating on a postage stamp, and the furniture never sits right. Three rules fix it.

Rule one: front legs on

In a living room, at least the front legs of every sofa and chair should sit on the rug. For a 3 m sofa, a 200 x 300 cm rug is the smallest size that works.

Rule two: leave 30 to 50 cm of floor

A border of bare floor frames the rug. Less than 30 cm and the rug looks squeezed. More than 50 cm and it starts to look small again.

Rule three: under the bed, not beside it

For a bed, the rug should extend at least 60 cm past both sides and the foot. A 160 x 230 rug suits a double, a 200 x 300 suits a king.
