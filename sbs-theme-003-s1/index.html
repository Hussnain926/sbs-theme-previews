    Questions, answered

    In what order do I apply the products?

    Thinnest to thickest: cleanser, mist or toner, serum, moisturiser. SPF goes on last in the morning. The routine builder lists the steps in order.

    Can I use niacinamide and vitamin C together?

    Yes. Our Glow Drops and Barrier Serum are made at a pH that lets you layer them. If your skin is sensitive, use one in the morning and one at night.

    Are the products safe during pregnancy?

    Our formulas do not contain retinol or salicylic acid above 0.5%. Ask your doctor before starting new skincare during pregnancy.

    How to layer a serum and a moisturiser

    A serum and a moisturiser do different jobs. The serum carries the active ingredient into the top layer of the skin. The moisturiser slows down water loss. Put them on in the wrong order and the serum cannot reach the skin.

    Thinnest to thickest

    Apply products from the most watery to the richest. On most mornings that means:

    1. Gentle cleanser
    2. Mist or toner, if you use one
    3. Serum: two to three drops
    4. Moisturiser: a pea-sized amount
    5. SPF 50: two finger lengths for face and neck
    Give each layer about a minute. It does not need to be fully dry, just no longer wet.

    One active at a time

    If you are new to actives, start with one serum and use it for four weeks before adding another. That way you can tell which product is doing what.
