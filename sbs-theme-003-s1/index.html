Yes. It is made without essential oils and alcohol. As with any new product, patch test first.

Can I use it with retinol?

Yes. Use the serum first, wait a minute, then apply your retinol and cream.

Is the scent strong?

The rose water gives a very light natural scent that fades within minutes.

Ritual · 6 min read

The evening ritual, step by step

By the Dewpoint team

Evening is when skin does most of its repair. A calm routine helps it along: melt away the day, add moisture back, then seal it in so you wake up soft.

i. Melt

Massage a balm into dry skin for a full minute. It lifts sunscreen and makeup without stripping, then rinses clean with warm water.

Cloud Balm100 ml · $38

ii. Mist & press

Mist rose water while skin is still damp, then press in a few drops of serum. Pressing instead of rubbing keeps sensitive skin calm.

"Skin loves the same calm routine, night after night."

iii. Seal

A rich cream locks everything in. Pat a little eye elixir under the eyes last.
