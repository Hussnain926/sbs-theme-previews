Guide · 6 min read

How a 3-layer shell works

// By Kai, product leadEntry 114

A three-layer shell bonds a waterproof membrane between a tough face fabric and a thin inner scrim. No loose lining, no hanging mesh. It is lighter, it dries faster and it lasts longer.

Face fabric40D nylon ripstop
MembraneePE, 20,000 mm
Breathability20,000 g/m²/24h
FinishPFC-free DWR

Why the DWR matters

The outer finish makes water bead and roll off. When it wears out, the face fabric soaks up water and the shell feels damp even though the membrane is still dry.

"Dry inside starts with water rolling off outside."

Wash it, tumble it low for twenty minutes, and the finish comes back.
