    How to pack a 38 litre bag for two nights out

    By the Switchback team · Sample article

    A well-packed bag carries lighter than the scale says. Heavy things close to your back, the things you need often at the top, and nothing hanging off the outside that can catch on a branch.

    Start with the base weight

    Base weight is everything except food, water and fuel. Weigh each item once and write it down. It is the fastest way to see where the grams are.

    <7 kgbase weight target
    3zones: bottom, core, top
    1 Lwater on the move
    Pack for the weather you will get, not the weather you hope for.

    Bottom, core, top

    Sleeping kit at the bottom. Food and stove in the core, against your spine. Shell, snacks and headlamp at the top where you can reach them in a minute.

    Check it on the scale

    Use the pack weight builder on our home page to add up your kit before you leave.
