Guides

The three-layer system, explained

By the Gauge test team · 7 min read

The secret to staying comfortable outside is not one warm jacket. It is three thin layers that you can add and remove as the day changes.

BaseMoves sweat away from skin
MidTraps warm air
ShellBlocks wind and rain

Start with merino

A merino base layer keeps you warm when damp and does not smell after a long day. Choose 150 for summer, 200 for most of the year.

"Be bold, start cold. You will warm up in ten minutes."

Add fleece or down as your mid layer, then a shell when the wind picks up or the rain starts.
