    How to choose a toy that lasts

    Most toys fail because they are the wrong match for the dog, not because they are badly made. Start with how your dog plays.

    Know your chewer

    Gentle chewers carry and cuddle. Average chewers chew for fun. Power chewers work at a toy until it gives. Pick the material to match.

    Plushfor gentle chewers and cats
    Ropefor tug and average chewers
    Rubberfor power chewers
    Size up when in doubt. A toy should never fit fully in the mouth.

    Check toys weekly

    Look for cracks, loose threads and small pieces. Replace any toy that starts to break apart.
