Repair · 5 min read

how to patch a jacket in 20 minutes

By Ines, Pinefield repair crew

Most tears are smaller than they look. With a patch and a few stitches, your jacket is ready for another ten years.

you will need

  1. A fabric patch, a bit bigger than the tear
  2. Needle and strong thread
  3. Scissors and a few pins
  4. Twenty quiet minutes

start from the inside

Turn the jacket inside out. Pin the patch over the tear and sew around the edge with small, even stitches.

"the greenest jacket is the one you already own."

Too tricky? Send it to us. Repairs on Pinefield gear are free, for life.

About Pinefield

outdoor gear, without the guilt.
