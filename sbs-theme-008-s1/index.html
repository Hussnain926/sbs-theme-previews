    Ten puddle-proof rainy day ideas

    By the Tumble team·November 2026

    Children do not mind the rain nearly as much as grown-ups do. With the right layers, a wet afternoon can be the best one of the week.

    Dress for it

    A breathable base layer, a warm mid layer and a waterproof shell keep little ones happy for longer. Wellies one size up leave room for thick socks.

    There is no bad weather, only the wrong outfit.

    Our favourite games

    1. Puddle measuring with a stick and a ruler.
    2. Leaf boats in the gutter stream.
    3. Worm rescue, with a small bucket.
    4. Rain drawings with chalk.
    Tumble tipHang wet macs upside down by the hem so the hood dries first. They will be ready again by tea time.

    When everyone is soaked and smiling, swap into warm pyjamas and call it a day well spent.

    Common questions

    How do your sizes run?
    True to age with a little grow room. If your child is tall for their age, or between sizes, take the next size up and roll the cuffs.
    How does a free size swap work?
    Within 30 days, send the piece back with the return label from your account and we send the new size for free.
    Can I send back outgrown clothes?
    Yes. Send back any outgrown Tumble piece in wearable condition and get $5 store credit per piece.
    Do you gift wrap?
    Every order can be gift wrapped for free with a handwritten note. Tick the box in your bag.
