    How to run your first night 5K

    By the Kadence teamSample article

    Running after dark feels different. The streets are quieter, the air is cooler and the session becomes yours. It also needs a little more planning. Here is the simple build we use for runners who can only train after work.

    Week by week

    Start with three runs a week. Keep two of them easy, where you can talk in full sentences. Add one session with short faster blocks. Each week, add five minutes to the longest run.

    3×Runs per week
    +5Minutes each week
    1Faster session
    Easy runs make the fast days possible.

    Be seen

    Wear at least one light or reflective piece on the front and one on the back. Pick routes with street lights and a loop you know. Tell someone where you are going.

    What to wear

    Layer for the second kilometre, not the first. You should feel a little cool when you start. A light half-zip and tights cover most nights down to around 5°C.
