The story

Ambre Nuit was built for the walk home after dinner. Bergamot and pink pepper open it, a heart of orris and labdanum keeps it soft, and a base of vanilla and smoked vetiver stays on skin until morning.

Full ingredients

Alcohol denat., parfum (fragrance), aqua (water), limonene, linalool, coumarin, benzyl benzoate, citral, eugenol. Vegan. Made without phthalates.

How to wear

Two sprays on pulse points from 15 cm. Do not rub. For longer wear, layer over the Ambre Nuit body oil.

Why perfume fades on some skin, and how to make it stay.

By the Ambrette studio

Perfume is a mix of oils dissolved in alcohol. When you spray it, the alcohol lifts off in seconds and the oils start to evaporate, light ones first. How fast that happens depends a lot on your skin.

Dry skin has less to hold on to, so the top notes vanish quickly and even the base can feel thin by lunch. Warm skin projects more but also burns through a scent faster.

Give it something to cling to

The simplest fix is moisture. Shower with a wash in the same accord, then press a few drops of body oil into damp skin. Spray on top while the skin is still warm.

Scent clings to moisture. Oil first, perfume second, and never rub your wrists together.

Where to spray

Pulse points are warm, so they lift the scent towards you: inner wrists, the base of the throat, behind the ears and the backs of the knees. Hair holds scent well too, but alcohol can dry it, so mist a brush instead.

Do not rub. Rubbing heats the skin and breaks up the top notes before you get to smell them.

Why ambrette

Named for a seed that smells of skin.

Ambrette seed gives a soft, warm musk that sits close to the body. It is in every scent we make, a small signature you notice only up close.

We start from the evening: what you want to smell like when the day is over. Everything else follows from that.
