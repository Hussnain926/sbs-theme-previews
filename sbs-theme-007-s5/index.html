    How to care for a leather collar

    Full-grain leather is the top layer of the hide. It is the strongest part, and it changes over time. Dark spots and soft creases are not damage. They are the collar learning the shape of the neck it lives on.

    Clean it gently

    Wipe off mud with a damp cloth and let the collar dry flat, away from a radiator. Never put leather in the washing machine, and never dry it in the sun.

    Do

    • Wipe with a damp cloth
    • Dry flat at room temperature
    • Feed with balm twice a year

    Avoid

    • Soaking or swimming
    • Radiators and direct sun
    • Harsh soaps and sprays
    Leather is skin. Treat it like your own and it will last for years.

    Feed it twice a year

    A thin layer of leather balm in spring and autumn keeps the fibres supple. Rub it in with a soft cloth, wait an hour, then buff. Check the stitching and the buckle at the same time.
