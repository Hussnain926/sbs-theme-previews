The happiest pour over: 15 g, 250 g, 3 minutes

By Mia, head roaster6 min readUpdated October 2026

Pour over is the easiest way to taste what makes a coffee special. You need a dripper, a scale and three calm minutes.

Recipe card · one mug
15 gCoffee
250 gWater
94°CTemp
3:00Time

What you need

A dripper with a paper filter, a kettle (gooseneck if you have one), a scale and coffee roasted in the last few weeks. Grind it like coarse sand.

The steps

  1. Rinse the filter with hot water and tip that water away.
  2. Add 15 g of coffee and give it a gentle shake to flatten.
  3. Pour 40 g of water and wait 30 seconds while it blooms.
  4. Pour slowly in small circles until the scale reads 250 g.
  5. Let it drain and aim to finish around three minutes.
Sour? Grind finer. Bitter? Grind coarser. That's it.

Which coffee to use

Fruity coffees love this method. Try Berry Pop for raspberry and cola, or Butter Cup if you like it sweet and round.
