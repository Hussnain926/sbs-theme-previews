How to keep cashmere for twenty years

Words by Marta, head of knitwear

Cashmere yarn at the Porto atelier

Good cashmere gets better with age. The fibres bloom, the knit softens and the piece starts to take your shape. All it asks is a little patience.

Wash less than you think

Airing a knit overnight does most of the work. When it does need a wash, use cold water and a wool soap, press out the water in a towel and dry it flat.

"Fold it, never hang it. Rest it a day between wears."

Pilling is normal in the first weeks. Use a cashmere comb, gently, and it will settle. And if anything goes wrong, our ten-year repair promise has you covered.
