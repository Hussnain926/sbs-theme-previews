Why we test fleece forty times

12 September 2026 · By Sam Hale · 5 min read

A fleece can feel perfect on day one and fall apart by week six. We wanted to know why, so every new fabric goes through forty home-style washes before we cut a single piece.

Washes one to ten

This is where shrink shows. Our first loopback lost 6% in length. We pre-shrunk the cloth at the mill and the loss dropped under 2%.

Washes ten to thirty

Pilling starts here. Tighter yarn twist and a single brushing pass kept the surface flat where a double brush fuzzed up within a dozen washes.

Washes thirty to forty

By now the cuffs tell the truth. Ours still spring back. If they hadn't, the fabric wouldn't have made the drop.

Made to move

Voltline started in 2020 in a Sheffield garage with one hoodie and a simple test: wash it forty times and see what's left.

Most of what we tried fell apart, so we went to the mills directly and asked for heavier cloth, tighter knits and finishes that last. We make small runs and repair what we sell.

The cloth

Loopback from Portugal, jersey from Turkey, shell fabric from Italy. Every mill is audited before we order.

The cut

Patterns are cut for movement: gusseted shoulders, longer backs, and room where lifters need it.

The finish

Flat-lock seams and bar-tacked stress points. Built so the repair promise rarely gets used.
