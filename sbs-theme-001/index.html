The first six months in raw denim

By Ken, head cutter

Raw denim starts stiff and dark. Over months of wear it softens and fades where your body creases it. Here is how to get the best from your first pair.

Step 01Size snug

The waist stretches about an inch.

Step 02Wear hard

Five days a week, for six months.

Step 03First soak

Cold water, inside out, hang dry.

Why wait to wash

Indigo sits on the surface of the yarn. The longer you wear before the first soak, the sharper the contrast between the creases and the rest of the leg.

"Wear them like you mean it. The jeans will remember."

Use our soak planner to work out your first soak date from the day you started wearing them.

How should raw denim fit?

Snug in the waist. Raw denim stretches about an inch, so size down if you are between sizes.

Is hemming really free?

Yes. Choose your inseam and stitch on the product page or in the hemming service. Hemmed pairs can be exchanged but not refunded.

When should I wash them?

Wait about six months of regular wear. Use the soak planner to find your date.
