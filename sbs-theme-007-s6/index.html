Five ridge walks your dog will love.

By the Cairn test packOctober 2026

A good ridge walk for a dog is not the one with the best view. It is the one with water on the way up, shade at the top and nothing your dog has to be lifted over. These five tick all three.

1. Start early, finish before the heat

Rock holds heat. By early afternoon a granite slab can be too hot for bare pads, so we plan to be on the way down by noon in summer, and carry booties for the last stretch.

Pack water for two. Your dog drinks more than you think above the treeline.

3. Read the trail

Loose scree, long ladders and narrow ledges are where dogs get hurt. If a route needs a hand on the rock, it is not a dog route. Turn around, there is always another ridge.

About Cairn

We make gear for the dogs who lead the way.

A cairn is a stack of stones that marks the trail for whoever comes next. We started Cairn after one too many harnesses rubbed, slipped or snapped halfway up a mountain.

Today a small team of designers and a test pack of eleven dogs make every harness, lead and pack we sell. Nothing reaches the shop until it has done a full season outside.

Quick answers

Before you ask.

How do I measure chest girth? +
Wrap a soft tape around the widest part of the rib cage, just behind the front legs. Keep it snug, not tight, and use the fit finder for the size.
What if the size is wrong? +
Exchanges for a different size are free within 60 days, even if you have been out on the trail.
What does the warranty cover? +
All buckles, clips and hardware for life. Straps and fabric for two years of normal use.
