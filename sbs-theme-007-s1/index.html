    Your puppy's first walk: a happy, calm start

    By the Wagtail team · Sample article

    The first walks are about smells, sounds and trust, not distance. Keep them short, keep them happy and let your puppy explore at their own pace.

    Harness or collar?

    A padded harness spreads pressure across the chest, which is kinder for small necks while your puppy learns not to pull. Keep a collar on for the ID tag.

    5 minper month of age, twice a day
    2fingers of room under the strap
    1treat for every calm check-in
    Short, sniffy and fun beats long and tiring.

    Bring the right kit

    A light lead, a pocket of small treats, a bottle of water and bags. Use the size finder on our home page to get the harness fit right.
